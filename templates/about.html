Our Mission

Daiva Marga brings the ancient science of Vedic astrology to the digital age. Using precise astronomical calculations and traditional techniques, we provide accurate horoscopes for self-discovery and guidance.

The Technology

Our calculations are powered by the Swiss Ephemeris library, ensuring high precision in planetary positions. We use the Lahiri ayanamsa for sidereal calculations, following traditional Vedic methods.
